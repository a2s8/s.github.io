The Magic Chameleon

A small color-changing toy that hides a surprisingly good science lesson inside a quick water experiment.

View the toy on Amazon More from Avik

What we discovered

A playful object with a real material-science behavior.

How it works

The paint uses thermochromic pigments that react to temperature, making the chameleon appear to camouflage in water.

Where it came from

It arrived through a class Valentine's Day exchange and quickly turned into a simple at-home experiment.

Try it carefully

Compare cool and warm water, watch the transition, and let the toy return to room temperature between tests.
